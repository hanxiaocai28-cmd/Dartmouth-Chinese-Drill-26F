Part 3 · 新句子 New sentences

Three new sentences

一
你今天过得怎么样？
Nǐ jīntiān guò de zěnmeyàng?
How has your day been?
二
我很好，你呢？
Wǒ hěn hǎo, nǐ ne?
I’m good. And you?
三
李老师是美国老师，我也是美国学生。
Lǐ lǎoshī shì Měiguó lǎoshī, wǒ yě shì Měiguó xuésheng.
Teacher Li is an American teacher, and I’m also an American student.

Pieces to notice

Small words doing big jobs

过得guò de

Verb + 得 describes how something went. 过得怎么样？ asks how your day, or life, is going.

呢ne

Bounces the same question back without repeating it: 我很好，你呢？

也yě

“Also.” It sits right before the verb, never at the end: 我也是美国学生。

Part 4 · 对话 Dialogue

Practice with a partner

Run it twice and swap roles. B fills in their own name.

A
你好！请问你是美国学生吗？
Nǐ hǎo! Qǐngwèn nǐ shì Měiguó xuésheng ma?
Hello! May I ask, are you an American student?
B
不，我是中国学生，你呢？
Bù, wǒ shì Zhōngguó xuésheng, nǐ ne?
No, I’m a Chinese student. And you?
A
我是美国学生。你叫什么名字？
Wǒ shì Měiguó xuésheng. Nǐ jiào shénme míngzi?
I’m an American student. What’s your name?
B
我叫，我的老师是李老师，他是中国老师。
Wǒ jiào ____, wǒ de lǎoshī shì Lǐ lǎoshī, tā shì Zhōngguó lǎoshī.
My name is ____. My teacher is Teacher Li; he’s a Chinese teacher.
