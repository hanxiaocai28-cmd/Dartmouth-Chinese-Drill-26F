P4 · 对话 Dialogue · Round 2

对话练习：看英文说中文

Same dialogue, same order. Look at the English and say each line in Chinese.

A
Are you busy today?
B
I’m very busy today. I have three midterms.
A
OK then, may I treat you to coffee?
B
No, I don’t like drinking coffee. I like drinking tea.
A
OK then, do you prefer Chinese tea or British tea?
B
I prefer drinking Chinese tea.
A
Umm, but I only have British tea. Sorry.
B
Never mind. I’ll have coffee.
