The Four Tones 四声调

一

First tone mā 妈

High and flat — hold it level, like singing one long note.

二

Second tone má 麻

Rising — like asking “what?” in English.

三

Third tone mǎ 马

Dips down, then rises — the low, scooping tone.

四

Fourth tone mà 骂

Sharp fall — short and firm, like a command.

Pitch contours of the four tones (5 = high, 1 = low). We write them with the numbers 1 2 3 4.

New · 学习新句子

Saying Who You Are 学习新句子

我是美国人。 Wǒ shì Měiguó rén. I am American. — swap in 中国 / 越南 / 加拿大 / 柬埔寨
你好，请问，你是老师吗？ Nǐ hǎo, qǐngwèn, nǐ shì lǎoshī ma? Hello — may I ask, are you a teacher?
不是，我不是老师，我是学生。 Bú shì, wǒ bú shì lǎoshī, wǒ shì xuésheng. No — I'm not a teacher, I'm a student.
是

shì — to be

Country + 人 rén = a person from there: 美国人, 中国人, 越南人…

吗

ma — question particle

Statement + 吗 = yes/no question. 你是老师 → 你是老师吗？

不

bù — not

不 + 是 = “is not”. Before 是 it is pronounced bú.

请

qǐngwèn — “may I ask…”

Polite opener before a question, like “excuse me”.
