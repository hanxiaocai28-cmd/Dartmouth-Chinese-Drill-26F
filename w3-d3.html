P3 · 新句子 New sentences

学习新句子

New words are in red. Read first, then check the pinyin and English.

一
你Nǐ家jiā有yǒu几jǐ口kǒu人rén？
How many people are in your family?
二
这Zhè是shì你nǐ的de妈mā妈ma还hái是shi爸bà爸ba？
Is this your mom or your dad?
三
你Nǐ的de妈mā妈ma做zuò什shén么me工gōng作zuò？
What does your mom do for work?
四
我Wǒ的de爸bà爸ba是shì律lǜ师shī。
My dad is a lawyer.

新词

New words

  • 家jiā
    family; home
  • 几jǐ
    how many (a small number)
  • 口kǒu
    measure word for family members
  • 做zuò
    to do
  • 工gōng作zuò
    job; work
  • 律lǜ师shī
    lawyer

口 counts family members only:
我家有四口人。
A question with 还是 takes no 吗.
