P3 · 新句子 New sentences

学习新句子

New words are in red. Read first, then check the pinyin and English.

一
今Jīn天tiān是shì星xīng期qī一yī。
Today is Monday.
二
昨Zuó天tiān是shì星xīng期qī天tiān，后hòu天tiān是shì星xīng期qī三sān。
Yesterday was Sunday, and the day after tomorrow is Wednesday.
三
你Nǐ今jīn年nián多duō大dà？
How old are you this year?
四
你Nǐ喜xǐ欢huan早zǎo上shang还hái是shi晚wǎn上shang？
Do you like mornings or evenings better?

A
