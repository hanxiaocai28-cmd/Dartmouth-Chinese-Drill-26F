复习句子

Read each sentence aloud first. Pinyin comes next, then English, then answer the question.

一
我Wǒ想xiǎng问wèn您nín几jǐ个ge问wèn题tí。
I’d like to ask you a few questions.
二
您Nín什shén么me时shí候hou有yǒu空kòng？
When will you be free?
三
明Míng天tiān四sì点diǎn以yǐ后hòu才cái有yǒu空kòng。
I won’t be free until after four tomorrow.

P3 · 新句子 New sentences

学习新句子

New words are in red. Read first, then check the pinyin and English.

一
你Nǐ帮bāng我wǒ准zhǔn备bèi一yí下xià，跟gēn我wǒ练liàn习xí说shuō中Zhōng文wén，好hǎo吗ma？
Could you help me prepare and practise speaking Chinese with me?
二
好Hǎo啊a，但dàn是shì你nǐ得děi请qǐng我wǒ喝hē咖kā啡fēi。
Sure, but you have to treat me to coffee.
三
我Wǒ回huí来lái以yǐ后hòu给gěi你nǐ打dǎ电diàn话huà。
I’ll call you after I get back.
四
好Hǎo，我wǒ等děng你nǐ的de电diàn话huà。
OK, I’ll wait for your call.

P4 · 对话 Dialogue · Round 1 · 1/2

对话练习：照着读

Read both pages, then swap roles.

A
你Nǐ今jīn天tiān忙máng吗ma？
B
我Wǒ今jīn天tiān很hěn忙máng，我wǒ下xià个ge星xīng期qī要yào考kǎo中Zhōng文wén。
A
我Wǒ跟gēn你nǐ练liàn习xí说shuō中Zhōng文wén，好hǎo吗ma？
B
好Hǎo啊a！你nǐ今jīn天tiān晚wǎn上shang有yǒu空kòng儿r吗ma？

空儿 = kòngr · Say the final -r together, not as a separate syllable.

P5 · 互动游戏 Find a time

我们什么时候一起去？

Play A and B. Ask about a one-hour slot; explain conflicts, then agree on a plan.

Plan together:
你Nǐ星xīng期qī几jǐ有yǒu时shí间jiān？
我Wǒ没méi空kòng，因yīn为wèi我wǒ要yào……
Choose a slot on either calendar. Role-play first, then check whether you are both free.

Practice schedules, not real dates · Empty cells = free · Every slot lasts one hour · Space: check / hide · N: next pair · Arrows: slides
