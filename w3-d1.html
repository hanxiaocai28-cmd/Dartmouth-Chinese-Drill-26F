Part 3 · 新词 · New words

Grandparents on both sides

一
爷yé爷ye

Grandpa on Dad's side

二
奶nǎi奶nai

Grandma on Dad's side

三
外wài公gōng

Grandpa on Mom's side

四
外wài婆pó

Grandma on Mom's side

外wài means “outside.” It marks the grandparents on Mom's side of the family.

Part 3 · 新句子 · New sentences

Saying who someone is

一
王Wáng朋Péng是shì我wǒ的de儿ér子zi，
也yě是shì我wǒ的de朋péng友you。
Wang Peng is my son, and also my friend.
二
那Nà个ge女nǚ孩hái是shì谁shéi？
Who is that girl?
三
那Nà个ge女nǚ孩hái姓xìng什shén么me？
What is that girl's surname?

New words

From the sentences

儿ér子zison 也yěalso, too 朋péng友youfriend 那nà个gethat (one) 女nǚ孩háigirl 谁shéiwho 姓xìngto be surnamed
是谁 asks who someone is. 姓什么 asks only for the family name, so the answer looks like 她姓王。
