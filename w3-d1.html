Grandparents on both sides

一
爷爷yéye

Grandpa on Dad's side

二
奶奶nǎinai

Grandma on Dad's side

三
外公wàigōng

Grandpa on Mom's side

四
外婆wàipó

Grandma on Mom's side

外 wài means “outside.” It marks the grandparents on Mom's side of the family.

Part 3 · 新句子 · New sentences

Saying who someone is

一
王朋是我的儿子，也是我的朋友。
Wáng Péng shì wǒ de érzi, yě shì wǒ de péngyou.
Wang Peng is my son, and also my friend.
二
那个女孩是谁？
Nàge nǚhái shì shéi?
Who is that girl?
三
那个女孩姓什么？
Nàge nǚhái xìng shénme?
What is that girl's surname?

New words

From the sentences

儿子érzison 也yěalso, too 朋友péngyoufriend 那个nàgethat (one) 女孩nǚháigirl 谁shéiwho 姓xìngto be surnamed
是谁 asks who someone is. 姓什么 asks only for the family name, so the answer looks like 她姓王。
