P2 · 复习 Review

复习句子

Read each sentence aloud first. Pinyin comes next, then English, then answer the question.

一
你nǐ上shàng个gè星xīng期qī考kǎo试shì考kǎo得de怎zěn么me样yàng？
How did you do on last week’s exam?
二
因yīn为wèi你nǐ帮bāng我wǒ复fù习xí，所suǒ以yǐ考kǎo得de不bú错cuò。
Because you helped me review, I did pretty well.
三
你nǐ写xiě字zì写xiě得de真zhēn好hǎo，真zhēn快kuài。
You write characters really well, and really fast.
四
你nǐ明míng天tiān有yǒu中zhōng文wén课kè吗ma？
Do you have Chinese class tomorrow?

P4 · 对话 Dialogue · Round 1

对话练习：照着读

Read the complete dialogue, then swap roles.

A
你nǐ今jīn天tiān忙máng吗ma？
B
我wǒ今jīn天tiān很hěn忙máng，我wǒ下xià个gè星xīng期qī要yào考kǎo中zhōng文wén。
A
第dì七qī课kè的de语yǔ法fǎ怎zěn么me样yàng？
B
语yǔ法fǎ很hěn容róng易yì，可kě是shì生shēng词cí太tài多duō。
A
你nǐ懂dǒng这zhè些xiē汉hàn字zì吗ma？
B
我wǒ不bù懂dǒng，你nǐ教jiāo我wǒ，可kě以yǐ吗ma？
A
没méi问wèn题tí。给gěi我wǒ你nǐ的de课kè本běn。
B
谢xiè谢xiè！我wǒ们men一yì起qǐ预yù习xí吧ba。

P4 · 对话 Dialogue · Round 2

对话练习：看英文说中文

Same dialogue, same order. Say the Chinese from these English cues.

A
Are you busy today?
B
I am busy today. I have a Chinese exam next week.
A
What is the grammar in Lesson Seven like?
B
The grammar is easy, but there are too many new words.
A
Do you understand these Chinese characters?
B

A
No problem. Give me your textbook.
B
Thanks! Let’s prepare for class together.

P5 · 互动游戏 Find a time

我们什么时候一起去？

Play A and B. Ask about a one-hour slot; explain conflicts, then agree on a plan.

Plan together:

我Wǒ没méi空kòng，因yīn为wèi我wǒ要yào……
Choose a slot on either calendar. Role-play first, then check whether you are both free.
