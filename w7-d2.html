P2 · 复习 Review

复习句子

Read each sentence aloud first. Pinyin comes next, then English, then answer the question.

一
李Lǐ老lǎo师shī，今jīn天tiān下xià午wǔ您nín有yǒu时shí间jiān吗ma？
Teacher Li, do you have time this afternoon?
二
今Jīn天tiān下xià午wǔ我wǒ没méi空kòng，因yīn为wèi我wǒ要yào开kāi会huì
I’m not free this afternoon because I have to attend a meeting.
三
我Wǒ今jīn天tiān在zài学xué校xiào的de办bàn公gōng室shì等děng你nǐ
I’ll wait for you in the school office today.
四
我Wǒ下xià个ge星xīng期qī有yǒu中Zhōng文wén考kǎo试shì
I have a Chinese exam next week.

P3 · 新句子 New sentences

学习新句子

New words are in red. Read first, then check the pinyin and English.

一
我Wǒ想xiǎng问wèn您nín几jǐ个ge问wèn题tí。
I’d like to ask you a few questions.
二
您Nín什shén么me时shí候hou有yǒu空kòng儿r？
When will you be free?
三
明Míng天tiān四sì点diǎn以yǐ后hòu才cái有yǒu空kòng儿r。
I won’t be free until after four tomorrow.
四
要Yào是shi您nín方fāng便biàn，四sì点diǎn半bàn我wǒ到dào您nín的de办bàn公gōng室shì去qù，行xíng吗ma？
If it’s convenient for you, may I come to your office at four-thirty?

P5 · 互动游戏 Find a time

我们什么时候一起去？

Play A and B. Ask about a one-hour slot; explain conflicts, then agree on a plan.

Plan together:
你Nǐ星xīng期qī几jǐ有yǒu时shí间jiān？
我Wǒ没méi空kòng，因yīn为wèi我wǒ要yào……
Choose a slot on either calendar. Role-play first, then check whether you are both free.

Practice schedules, not real dates · Empty cells = free · Every slot lasts one hour · Space: check / hide · N: next pair · Arrows: slides
