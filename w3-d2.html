P3 · 新句子 New sentences

学习新句子

New words are in red. Read first, then check the pinyin and English.

一
安Ān哲Zhé宇yǔ没méi有yǒu哥gē哥ge，但dàn是shì他tā有yǒu妹mèi妹mei。
An Zheyu doesn’t have an older brother, but he has a younger sister.
二
我Wǒ没méi有yǒu儿ér子zi，我wǒ也yě不bù想xiǎng有yǒu儿ér子zi。
I don’t have a son, and I don’t want one either.
三
这Zhè张zhāng照zhào片piàn是shì谁shéi的de？
Whose photo is this?
四
那Nà个ge男nán孩hái姓xìng什shén么me？
What is that boy’s surname?

新词

New words

  • 没méi有yǒu
    to not have
  • 但dàn是shì
    but
  • 想xiǎng
    to want to
  • 这zhè
    this
  • 张zhāng
    measure word for flat things
  • 照zhào片piàn
    photo
  • 谁shéi的de
    whose

有 is negated with 没, never 不:
我没有哥哥。　这张照片是谁的？ → 是我的。
