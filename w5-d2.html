复习句子

Read each sentence aloud first. Pinyin comes next, then English, then answer the question.

一
你Nǐ喜xǐ不bu喜xǐ欢huan看kàn电diàn影yǐng？
Do you like watching movies?

我Wǒ喜xǐ欢huan看kàn电diàn影yǐng。

P3 · 新句子 New sentences

学习新句子

New words are in red. Read first, then check the pinyin and English.

一
这Zhè周zhōu末mò我wǒ去qù跳tiào舞wǔ，你nǐ去qù不bu去qù？
This weekend I’m going dancing. Are you going?
二
你Nǐ今jīn天tiān晚wǎn上shang想xiǎng不bu想xiǎng去qù吃chī中Zhōng国guó菜cài？
Tonight, do you want to go and eat Chinese food?
三
你Nǐ最zuì喜xǐ欢huan的de电diàn影yǐng叫jiào什shén么me？
What is your favourite movie called?
四
你Nǐ最zuì喜xǐ欢huan在zài哪nǎ里lǐ逛guàng街jiē？
Where do you like to go window-shopping the most?

去不去, 想不想: verb + 不 + verb asks a yes/no question, just like 喜不喜欢.

最 + verb = “the most”. 你最喜欢……？ asks for a favourite.

P4 · 对话 Dialogue · Round 1

对话练习：照着读

Work in pairs. Read the Chinese and pinyin with your own names in the blanks, then swap roles.

A
你Nǐ好hǎo！你nǐ叫jiào什shén么me名míng字zi？
B
我Wǒ叫jiào，你nǐ呢ne？
A
我Wǒ叫jiào。你nǐ今jīn天tiān忙máng吗ma？
B
我Wǒ今jīn天tiān不bù忙máng，但dàn是shì我wǒ明míng天tiān忙máng。
A
我Wǒ今jīn天tiān晚wǎn上shang请qǐng你nǐ去qù看kàn电diàn影yǐng好hǎo吗ma？
B
好Hǎo，但dàn是shì为wèi什shén么me？
A
因Yīn为wèi你nǐ常cháng常cháng请qǐng我wǒ吃chī饭fàn，所suǒ以yǐ今jīn天tiān我wǒ请qǐng你nǐ看kàn电diàn影yǐng。
B
好Hǎo吧ba，我wǒ们men去qù看kàn电diàn影yǐng，但dàn你nǐ是shì我wǒ的de朋péng友you，不bú是shì我wǒ的de男nán朋péng友you。

关键词 Key words

好hǎo吧ba
OK, fine
男nán朋péng友you
boyfriend
去qù
to go
常cháng常cháng
often
因yīn为wèi……所suǒ以yǐ
because… so…

P4 · 对话 Dialogue · Round 2

对话练习：看英文说中文

Same dialogue, same order. Look at the English and say each line in Chinese.

A
Hi! What’s your name?
B
My name is . And you?
A
My name is . Are you busy today?
B
I’m not busy today, but I’m busy tomorrow.
A
Tonight let me treat you to a movie, OK?
B
OK, but why?
A
Because you often treat me to meals, today I’m treating you to a movie.
B
OK then, let’s go to the movie. But you’re my friend, not my boyfriend.
