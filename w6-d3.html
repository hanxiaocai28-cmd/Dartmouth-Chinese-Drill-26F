P3 · 新句子 New sentences

学习新句子

New words are in red. Read first, then check the pinyin and English.

一
我Wǒ在zài这zhè个ge大dà学xué学xué中Zhōng文wén。
I study Chinese at this university.
二
我Wǒ不bù喜xǐ欢huan在zài家jiā看kàn电diàn影yǐng。
I don’t like watching movies at home.
三
今Jīn天tiān妈mā妈ma喝hē了le三sān杯bēi水shuǐ。
Mom had three glasses of water today.

Read diǎn + er as diǎnr; liáo tiān + er as liáotiānr. The -r ending is not a separate syllable.

P3 · 新句子 New sentences

学习新句子

New words are in red. Read first, then check the pinyin and English.

一
你Nǐ喝hē了le几jǐ杯bēi水shuǐ？我wǒ喝hē了le一yì杯bēi水shuǐ。

B

A

B
去Qù了le，我wǒ们men一yì起qǐ聊liáo天tiān、看kàn电diàn视shì。
A
你Nǐ喝hē了le几jǐ杯bēi水shuǐ？
B
我Wǒ喝hē了le两liǎng杯bēi水shuǐ。你nǐ呢ne？
A
我Wǒ喝hē了le一yì杯bēi茶chá。你nǐ几jǐ点diǎn回huí家jiā？
B
我Wǒ晚wǎn上shang十shí二èr点diǎn才cái回huí家jiā。

Key words

在zài家jiā
at home (before the action)
喝hē了le
drank (completed action)
几jǐ杯bēi
how many cups / glasses
一yì起qǐ
together
才cái回huí家jiā
did not go home until …
