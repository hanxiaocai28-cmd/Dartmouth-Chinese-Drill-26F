声调 · The Four Tones

One syllable, four meanings

The pitch you use changes the word. Think of it as singing, not speaking.

  • 55 · 一声
    高平
    High and level — hold a steady, even note.
  • 35 · 二声
    上扬
    Rising — like the end of a surprised "huh?"
  • 214 · 三声
    先降后升
    Dips low, then lifts — a valley of pitch.
  • 51 · 四声
    下降
    A firm drop — like a quick, decisive "stop!"
