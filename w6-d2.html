P2 · 复习 Review

复习句子

Read each sentence aloud first. Pinyin comes next, then English, then answer the question.

一
我Wǒ不bù喜xǐ欢huan喝hē茶chá，我wǒ只zhǐ喝hē水shuǐ
I don’t like drinking tea. I only drink water.
二
你Nǐ最zuì喜xǐ欢huan喝hē什shén么me？
What do you like to drink the most?
三
你Nǐ在zài哪nǎ里lǐ工gōng作zuò？
Where do you work?
四
很Hěn高gāo兴xìng认rèn识shi你nǐ！
Nice to meet you!
五
王Wáng朋Péng家jiā很hěn大dà，很hěn漂piào亮liang，但dàn是shì没méi有yǒu可kě乐lè
Wang Peng’s home is big and beautiful, but there is no cola.

P3 · 新句子 New sentences

学习新句子

New words are in red. Read first, then check the pinyin and English.

一
请Qǐng进jìn，请qǐng进jìn，快kuài进jìn来lái！
Please come in. Please come in. Come in quickly!
二
是Shì吗ma？请qǐng坐zuò，请qǐng坐zuò。
Is that so? Have a seat, please.
三
你Nǐ们men想xiǎng喝hē点diǎn儿er什shén么me？喝hē茶chá还hái是shi喝hē咖kā啡fēi？
What would you like to drink? Tea or coffee?

Read diǎn + er as diǎnr; liáo tiān + er as liáotiānr. The -r ending is not a separate syllable.

P3 · 新句子 New sentences

学习新句子

New words are in red. Read first, then check the pinyin and English.

一
我Wǒ要yào一yì瓶píng可kě乐lè，可kě以yǐ吗ma？
I’d like a bottle of cola. Is that OK?
二
那Nà给gěi我wǒ一yì杯bēi水shuǐ吧ba。
Then please give me a glass of water.
三
你Nǐ喝hē一yì点diǎn儿er茶chá吧ba。
Have a little tea.

Read diǎn + er as diǎnr; liáo tiān + er as liáotiānr. The -r ending is not a separate syllable.

P4 · 对话 Dialogue · Round 1

对话练习：照着读

Work in pairs. Read the Chinese and pinyin, then swap roles.

A
你Nǐ今jīn天tiān忙máng吗ma？
B
我Wǒ今jīn天tiān很hěn忙máng，我wǒ有yǒu三sān个gemidterm
A
好Hǎo吧ba，那nà你nǐ来lái我wǒ家jiā喝hē一yì点diǎn儿er茶chá吧ba。
B
好Hǎo，谢xiè谢xie！
A
请Qǐng进jìn，请qǐng坐zuò。你nǐ想xiǎng喝hē茶chá还hái是shi喝hē咖kā啡fēi？
B
我Wǒ要yào一yì杯bēi茶chá，可kě以yǐ吗ma？
A
可Kě以yǐ，给gěi你nǐ。你nǐ在zài哪nǎ儿er工gōng作zuò？
B
我Wǒ在zài学xué校xiào的de图tú书shū馆guǎn工gōng作zuò。

Key words

请qǐng进jìn
please come in
请qǐng坐zuò
please have a seat
一yì点diǎn儿er

一yì杯bēi

可kě以yǐ
